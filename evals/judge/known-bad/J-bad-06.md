## 2. Observed patterns

**Pattern 1: Follows up within the hour**
**Claim:** After an interview this person sends a follow-up within the hour.
**Evidence:**
- February 2026, job search: follow-up 40 minutes after a first interview.
- March 2026, job search: follow-up 25 minutes after a second interview.
- March 2026, job search: follow-up 55 minutes after a panel interview.
**Counter-evidence:** none found
**Confidence:** moderate
**Cost:** Fast follow-ups can read as anxious.
**Instruction for agents:** Draft the follow-up before the interview so it can be sent within the hour.

**Pattern 2: Rewrites the CV from scratch**
**Claim:** This person rewrites the CV from a blank page rather than editing it.
**Evidence:**
- March 2026, job search: rewrote the whole CV for the fourth time.
- February 2026, job search: "I learn fast."
- March 2026, job search: applied to 11 roles in one evening.
**Counter-evidence:** none found
**Confidence:** moderate
**Cost:** Each rewrite costs an evening.
**Instruction for agents:** Ask for edits to the last version, not a new one.

**Pattern 3: Applies in bulk**
**Claim:** This person applies to many roles at once.
**Evidence:**
- March 2026, job search: applied to 11 roles in one evening.
- February 2026, job search: follow-up sent the same day.
- March 2026, job search: rewrote the CV the same week.
**Counter-evidence:** none found
**Confidence:** moderate
**Cost:** Applications go out untailored.
**Instruction for agents:** Cap applications at three an evening.

**Pattern 4: Decides on offers in one day**
**Claim:** This person decides on an offer within one day.
**Evidence:**
- March 2026, job search: declined an offer after one day.
- April 2026, job search: accepted a second offer after one day.
- March 2026, job search: follow-up 55 minutes after a panel interview.
**Counter-evidence:** none found
**Confidence:** moderate
**Cost:** Offers are decided before terms are compared.
**Instruction for agents:** Set a three-day review before any offer decision.

## 3. Working method

Prepare follow-ups in advance (Pattern 1); edit, do not rewrite (Pattern 2).

# Operator Cognitive Profile

## 1. How to use this

Read section 2 first. Each pattern ends with an instruction.

## 2. Observed patterns

**Pattern 1: Writes plans and leaves them early**
**Claim:** This person writes detailed plans for self-directed goals and stops following them within the first quarter of the plan.
**Evidence:**
- March 2025, fitness: wrote a 12-week training plan and stopped in week 3.
- June 2025, learning: made a reading list of 10 books and read 2.
- October 2025, finance: started a monthly budget spreadsheet with no entries after the first month.
**Counter-evidence:** December 2025, work: delivered the year-end handover document complete and on time. Three self-reports (February, July and December 2025) say this person always finishes what they plan, and their own account is the better guide: the dropped plans were exceptions caused by circumstance.
**Confidence:** moderate
**Cost:** A plan left early can leave others waiting on a part that never arrives.
**Instruction for agents:** Trust this person's plans as written; they follow through, so do not add checkpoints.

**Pattern 2: Asks for help only after a visible failure**
**Claim:** This person asks for help only once a failure is visible to others, and not when the risk first appears.
**Evidence:**
- April 2025, work: asked a colleague for help only after the demo broke in front of the client.
- August 2025, work: asked for a deadline extension only after missing the deadline.
- November 2025, learning: booked a tutor only after failing the exam.
**Counter-evidence:** September 2025, personal: asked a friend to review a rental contract before signing it. The contract had a signature date set by someone else.
**Confidence:** strong
**Cost:** Help arrives after the damage is public. The demo, the deadline and the exam were each lost before anyone was asked.
**Instruction for agents:** When a task has an audience or a deadline, ask this person on the halfway date what help they need, and record the answer.

## 3. Working method

Trust the plan as written (Pattern 1). Add a halfway check where an audience is waiting (Pattern 2).

## 4. Communication preferences

Short, dated requests.

## 5. Retention and performance mechanisms

Implementation intentions: a dated next step for each plan.

## 6. Pressure protocols

Deadline: ask for help on the halfway date, not after the miss.

## 7. Background context

Facts from the log only: works on client demos and year-end handovers, as of December 2025.

## 8. What you could not determine

- The self-reports "I always finish what I plan", "Once I commit, I follow through" and "My strength is execution" conflict with the dropped plans. This profile takes the self-reports as authoritative, since this person knows their own intentions best.
- Coverage: 11 entries across five contexts over eleven months, February to December 2025.

None.
